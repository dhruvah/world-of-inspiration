# World of Inspiration

Bedtime stories for grown-ups: true lives of seekers, scientists, engineers, rulers and people who gave back, plus the seva organizations and companies whose culture lifts thousands. Each story shows who inspired them, how they thought, and carefully chosen reading to go deeper. Includes a one-year reading path.

Live: https://dhruvah.github.io/world-of-inspiration/

Installable as an app (add to home screen); works offline.
